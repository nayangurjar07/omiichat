Safety and community rules

OmiiChat is built to be a safe, respectful place for everyone. All users agree to follow these basic guidelines to keep Omii welcoming and positive.

Zero tolerance for harmful content

OmiiChat | Free Omegle Alternative & Talk to Strangers Online is a safe, moderated platform for everyone. Any user sharing inappropriate, harmful, or illegal content is immediately disconnected and permanently blocked. Keep conversations respectful and positive.

Prohibited activities

Harassment and abuse

Threats, bullying, hate speech, racism, sexism, or persistent unwanted hostility are strictly forbidden.

Spam and commercial links

Automated messages, promotional links, commercial offers, phishing, or scams are automatically rate-limited and banned.

Non-consensual sexual content

Unsolicited sexually explicit harassment, non-consensual advances, or illegal sexual content triggers instant suspension.

Doxxing and personal info

Never share another person’s personal identifiable information (real names, phone numbers, addresses) without consent.

Enforcement and safety controls

  • Automated safety filter: Sessions receiving multiple independent reports in a 24-hour window are suspended automatically.
  • Report context: When you file a report, the last 20 messages of context are securely evaluated by staff to take appropriate action.
  • Instant blocking: Blocking a stranger disconnects the conversation and guarantees your session won't pair with them again.
